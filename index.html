From Inside the Industry

A Designer Who Built the Feed, Then Questioned It

Before founding Northlight, Ethan James Caldwell worked for roughly a decade as a product designer on consumer software, the kind of products measured by time-on-app, session depth, and return frequency. Those metrics shaped daily decisions. Over time, Caldwell concluded that they tracked how much attention a product extracted, not how much value it delivered.

That conclusion became the basis for Northlight, which he founded in 2021 around one measurable principle: a product should be judged by how much focus it returns to the people using it. The tool has since grown into a community of hundreds of thousands of members, a story told in this profile of the Northlight founder.

His 2024 book, The Attention Dividend, carries the argument into professional territory, treating sustained focus as an asset that compounds rather than a wellness habit. A fuller account of his path from product design shows why the argument draws on professional experience rather than theory.
